Section II

The Workshop

23 years on Ballarat roofs.

Vicwest Roofing started serving Ballarat and the surrounding region over two decades ago, during a period when many local roofing firms were small-scale operators without the capacity to handle larger commercial projects or multi-site work. The founders saw an opportunity to build a roofing company that could scale—taking on everything from residential re-roofs in Delacombe to full commercial roof replacements in Geelong—without sacrificing the responsiveness and quality that smaller operators prided themselves on. The business grew steadily through repeat clients and referrals, establishing workshops in both Ballarat and Geelong to cover the geographic spread between the Surf Coast, the Bellarine Peninsula, and the Central Highlands. Today, Vicwest Roofing operates as one of the largest roofing contractors in Western Victoria, with the infrastructure to manage projects across a 100km service radius while maintaining the same standards that built the business in the first place.

Vicwest Roofing specialises in three core services. Roof replacements are the primary offering—removing old tile, asbestos, or corrugated iron roofs and installing new Colorbond® or Zincalume® metal roofing systems with a 10-year workmanship warranty. This covers residential homes, rural sheds, and commercial buildings across Ballarat, Geelong, Bendigo, Bacchus Marsh, Daylesford, Castlemaine, Creswick, and Maryborough. New roof installation for new builds and extensions follows the same material standards, coordinating with builders to ensure weatherproofing is completed on schedule. Gutter and downpipe replacement rounds out the service list—fitting Colorbond® fascia, quad gutters, and downpipes with optional leaf guard systems to prevent blockages and water damage. All three services use the same crew, the same materials suppliers, and the same quality control process.

Vicwest Roofing employs a crew of licensed metal roofing tradespeople based out of workshops in Delacombe (Ballarat) and Geelong. The team includes roof plumbers, metal roofing installers, and labourers who've been with the business for multiple years—some over a decade. All tradespeople hold the necessary VBA (Victorian Building Authority) registration for roofing work over $10,000, and the business maintains its own fleet of vehicles, scaffolding, and safety equipment rather than relying on subcontractors. New hires go through on-site mentorship under senior tradespeople before working solo, and the business has posted job ads in recent years looking for additional metal roofing tradesmen to meet demand. The owner-operators maintain direct oversight of scheduling, quoting, and quality checks across both locations, ensuring consistency regardless of which crew is dispatched.

Vicwest Roofing has been operating for over 20 years, covering a service area that spans 100km from Ballarat in every direction—Geelong and the Bellarine Peninsula to the south, Bendigo to the north, Bacchus Marsh to the east, and Maryborough to the west. The business holds a 4.1-star rating across 18 Google reviews, reflecting real client feedback on completed projects. Based on review volume and tenure, the business has likely completed over 500 roof replacements and new installations since inception. Every roof replacement comes with a 10-year workmanship warranty, and all metal roofing is installed using Colorbond® or Zincalume® products from BlueScope Steel. The business operates two physical locations (Ballarat and Geelong) with dedicated workshops and equipment storage, allowing for faster response times and reduced travel costs across the region.

Vicwest Roofing doesn't subcontract—every project is completed by the company's own licensed tradespeople, which means accountability stays in-house from quote to final inspection. Site visits are scheduled within 48 hours of initial contact, and written quotes are provided within 24 hours of the visit, with no follow-up calls required unless the client has questions. For storm-damaged roofs or urgent leak repairs, the business prioritises rebooking site visits within one week rather than pushing clients to the end of the queue. Material orders are placed directly with BlueScope Steel and local suppliers, cutting out middlemen and ensuring consistent quality across every job. Projects are scheduled with buffer time built in—if a job is quoted at five days, it's planned for six, so weather delays or unforeseen issues don't cascade into the next client's timeline. The business maintains its own scaffolding and safety equipment rather than renting, which reduces setup time and allows for immediate response to safety concerns on-site.

Vicwest Roofing services Ballarat, Geelong, Bendigo, Bacchus Marsh, Daylesford, Castlemaine, Creswick, Maryborough, and all towns within 100km of Ballarat. To get a quote, call the Ballarat workshop or submit a request through the website form—both are monitored daily, and the business aims to respond within 24 hours. Site visits are free and include a roof inspection, measurements, and a discussion of material options (Colorbond® colour range, gutter profiles, leaf guard add-ons). The written quote provided after the visit breaks down labour, materials, and timeline with no hidden fees. If you're ready to proceed, the business schedules the job based on current workload and material lead times, typically within 2-4 weeks for standard residential re-roofs.
