How we work

Four steps. No surprises.

From first phone call to warranty handover, you know what's next.

  1. 1

    On-site assessment

    We climb the roof, photograph every plane, valley, and flashing. Measure to the metre. Talk you through what we found and what your options actually cost — not a salesperson reading from a script.

  2. 2

    Written quote within 48 hours

    Itemised: sheet metres of Colorbond, profile, colour, gutter linear metres, flashings, removal & tip fees. You can compare it line-for-line against any other quote.

  3. 3

    Schedule around your week

    Weather-aware booking · we don't start a strip-and-replace if rain is forecast inside the install window. Daily SMS update from the site supervisor while we're on your roof.

  4. 4

    Final walk + warranty pack

    10-year workmanship warranty in writing · BlueScope coating warranty registered · photo log of the completed job · cleanup signed off before invoice.
