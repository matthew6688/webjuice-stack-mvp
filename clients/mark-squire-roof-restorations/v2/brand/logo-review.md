# Logo Review — Mark Squire Roof Restorations

## Brief

- Business: Mark Squire Roof Restorations
- Niche: residential roof restoration
- Location: Ballarat, Victoria
- Tone: honest regional trade, owner-operator, workmanlike, heritage-credible (not luxury)
- Assumptions: no website exists; designed entirely as NEW logo. Owner-personal voice. Regional VIC (not metro Melbourne).

## Candidate Directions

1. **MS Apex Monogram** (selected) — Slab-serif M and S monogram where the M's center valley is exaggerated into a steel-blue ridge-cap apex (Λ shape). Stacked with "MARK SQUIRE" slab-serif wordmark and "ROOF RESTORATIONS" subtitle.
   - Lockup: stacked monogram over wordmark
   - Type: Roboto Slab 800/500
   - Palette: dark navy-charcoal + corrugated-steel blue-gray + warm cream
   - Premium move: M-valley-as-ridge-cap is the ownable signature; restoration-specific (ridge cap = roofing detail) without clip-art
   - Risk: monograms can feel generic — countered with the unique color-pop apex

2. **Squire Crest (trade badge)** — Heraldic shield/crest with MS interlocking and "EST. YEAR" arc.
   - Rejected: too generic trade-cottage feel; without verified founding year the "EST." element would be made-up; shield silhouettes are clip-art saturated.

3. **Signature Line + Roof** — Stylized handwritten "Mark Squire" signature with thin roof line above.
   - Rejected: signature logos age poorly, don't scale to 16px, and are over-used for owner-operator brands. Owner authenticity should come from copy + photography, not the mark.

## Selected Direction

**MS Apex Monogram**. Strongest because the ridge-cap apex in the M's valley is a roofing-specific structural detail (not metaphor / not clip-art). Slab serif anchors regional trade credibility (Roboto Slab is open-source — accessible to deploy). Cream surface + steel blue makes the brand feel honest-regional rather than corporate.

## Revision Notes

- Round 1: M valley too shallow, didn't read as ridge-cap. Increased valley depth, added blue fill.
- Round 2: S originally over-stylized with extra curves; simplified to one S-bend with slab terminals.
- Round 3: Not run — accepted at Round 2.

## Mono Check

- Method: ridge-cap apex collapses to same color as M's outline — apex geometry still visible via shape but loses color signal.
- Dark mono: PASS — MS remains structurally clear; ridge-cap visible as inner V shape.
- Light mono: PASS.
- Notes: full-color is meaningfully stronger. Mono is acceptable for print / fax only.

## Final Notes

- Roboto Slab is an open-source slab serif (Google Fonts) — free to deploy commercially. Wordmark uses <text> elements; outline to paths for print fidelity.
- The S glyph is custom-drawn (not from a font); proportions intentional.
- The cream surface #FAF6EE is brand-defining — don't replace with plain white in marketing materials.
