What We Looked At

We ran your website through the same checks that Google uses to decide which businesses to show first in search results. We also looked at it the way your customers do—on a phone, on slow internet, in a hurry to find someone who can help with a roofing problem.

Here's what we found.

What's Already Working Well

You've built genuine trust

Your 5-star rating with 37 Google reviews is excellent. That level of consistent feedback tells potential customers you deliver quality work. This is the hardest asset to build, and you've already got it.

Professional first impression

Your website uses real roofing imagery and clearly communicates that you're a licensed, insured, family-owned business. The trust elements are there—they just need to be easier to see and act on.

Contact options are present

You've included both a phone number and a quote request option. The foundation is there; we just need to make these paths easier for rushed mobile visitors to spot and use.

What's Holding You Back

These aren't small cosmetic issues—each one is costing you real enquiries from people who are already on your website looking for help.

Your phone number looks cut off

What we saw: The blue phone button in your header shows "0426 366 7.." with the last digits visually truncated.

Why it matters: When someone needs urgent roof repairs and your phone number looks incomplete or broken, they'll assume the website isn't being looked after—and they'll call the next company instead. The phone is your most important conversion path; it needs to work perfectly.

Mobile visitors can't tap to call

What we saw: Your phone number isn't set up as a click-to-call link on mobile devices.

Why it matters: Most of your visitors are on phones. When they have to copy your number, switch to their phone app, paste it in, and dial—you lose a significant portion of them at every step. One tap should dial. Every extra step costs you calls.

Your headline is too long to scan quickly

What we saw: "Feel Safe Under a Roof That Lasts - Brisbane Roof Restoration with 10 Years of Worry-Free Protection!" spans three large lines.

Why it matters: When someone clicks through from Google, they decide in 3-4 seconds whether to stay or go back. A headline that takes effort to read slows them down. In that pause, many will bounce back to the search results and try the next company.

The "Get Free Quote" button isn't obvious enough

What we saw: Your quote button is a small white pill in the top corner, less prominent than the phone button beside it.

Why it matters: Many roofing customers prefer to get a quote in writing before they call. If they can't immediately see how to request one, they'll go to a competitor's site where it's clearer. The quote request should be just as visible as the phone number.

Too much information competing for attention

What we saw: Nine bullet points across two columns above the fold, including long lines like "Clear and Consistent Communication Every Step of the Way".

Why it matters: Visitors don't read every point—they scan for the three or four things that matter most (licensed, insured, emergency service, free quotes). When everything is emphasised, nothing stands out, and the path to action gets buried.

What Changes When We Fix This

More phone calls from mobile visitors. A visible, tappable phone number above the fold removes the biggest barrier between "I need help" and "I'm calling them now."

More quote requests. When the quote form is easy to spot and looks like the primary action, people who prefer email or need a written estimate before committing will fill it in instead of leaving.

Better visibility in Google and AI search. Right now, Google and tools like ChatGPT can't easily read key information from your site (your services, location, credentials). Fixing the technical setup means you show up more often when people search for "roof repairs Brisbane" or ask an AI assistant for recommendations.

Stronger trust on first impression. When your phone number displays correctly, your key credentials are front and centre, and the page is easy to navigate, visitors instinctively trust that your actual roofing work will be just as professional.
