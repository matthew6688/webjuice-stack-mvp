Roof Replacement Experts - Serving Ballarat, Geelong, Bendigo & surrounding areas

Vic West Roofing is your local specialist for roof replacements in Ballarat and surrounding towns within 100km - including Geelong, Bendigo, Bacchus Marsh, Daylesford, and Castlemaine.

With over 20 years’ experience, we’ve helped homeowners and businesses replace ageing roofs, repair storm damage, and upgrade as part of major renovations. Our team delivers quality roofing solutions using premium materials, backed by exceptional workmanship and reliable turnaround times.

We handle everything from removing your old roof to installing a new Colorbond® or metal roofing system built to withstand Victorian weather. Whether it’s a small home or a large commercial building, we provide stress-free roof replacements - on time, on budget, and built to last.

Service Areas:
Ballarat | Geelong | Bendigo | Bacchus Marsh | Daylesford | Castlemaine | Creswick | Maryborough | and all towns within 100km of Ballarat.

OUR SERVICES

Roof Replacements﻿

Trusted roof replacement Ballarat & surrounds (100km). We remove your old roof and install durable Colorbond®/metal roofing with licensed installers, fast turnaround and a 10-year workmanship warranty for homes and commercial sites.

New Roofs

Expert new roof installation Ballarat, Geelong, Bendigo and regional towns. We design and fit Colorbond®/metal roofs for new builds and extensions, coordinating with your builder for a clean, on-schedule finish built to last.

Gutter & Pipe Replacement

Leak-free gutter replacement Ballarat and nearby areas. Supply and fit Colorbond® gutters, fascia and downpipes, plus leaf-guard options, to improve drainage and protect your property from water damage.
