Our work

Six things we do, all day, every day.

Replacements and restorations are the bulk of our calendar. Gutters, repairs and Colorbond fit-outs fill the rest. No tile we don't know.

Roof Replacement

Tile-to-metal switch or full Colorbond replace. Done in 3 to 5 days for most Ballarat homes, fully removed and recycled, with a 10-year written warranty on workmanship.

Roof Restoration

Pressure clean, re-bed, re-point, and recoat. Adds 10 to 15 years to an existing terracotta or cement-tile roof at a fraction of replacement cost.

Gutters & Downpipes

Quad, half-round, or box. Seamless aluminium or Colorbond, fitted with stainless brackets and leaf guard, paired with new PVC or metal downpipes.

Storm & Emergency Repairs

Hail, wind, or fallen-branch damage. We tarp same-day, photograph for your insurer, and replace tiles or sheeting once the claim's lodged.

Re-pointing & Re-bedding

Flexible pointing in 6 colours, replacing failed mortar bedding without disturbing tiles below. Stops leaks at the ridge, valleys, and hips.

Metal Roofing & Colorbond

Surfmist, Monument, Basalt. Full Colorbond install over timber battens, with anti-condensation blanket, insulation upgrade, and standing-seam detail at all junctions.

A Ballarat crew, on every Ballarat roof.

Vicwest Roofing started in 2003 out of a Delacombe shed. Twenty-two years on, we're still there. Our crew lives here, works here, and answers the phone here.

We don't subcontract. The person quoting your roof is the same person up there fitting it. That's how we keep the workmanship inside our 10-year written warranty, and why our insurance-storm jobs land on time.

Licensed with the Victorian Building Authority, fully insured, every job photographed start to finish.
