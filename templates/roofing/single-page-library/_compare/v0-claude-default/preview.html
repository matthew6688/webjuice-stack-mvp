Why Vicwest

22 years of Ballarat roofs.
Same family, same standard.

Vicwest Roofing started in Delacombe in 2003 with one ute, one ladder, and a strict rule: re-screw, re-bed, re-coat, or replace — but never paper over the problem.

Today the team runs three crews across Ballarat, Buninyong, and Bacchus Marsh. We do tile, metal, and the messy bits in between (valley flashings, ridge re-points, the gutter your previous trade said "wasn't worth doing"). Every job carries a 10-year written warranty, VBA-licensed sign-off, and the same number you called on day one.

We don't subcontract. The person who quotes you climbs your roof.
