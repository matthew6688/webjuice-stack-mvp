Our work

Six things we do, every week, in Ballarat.

Replacement, restoration, gutters, storm response, re-pointing, and Colorbond. No subcontractors, no surprises - the same crew quotes the job, finishes the job, and signs the warranty.

01 / Replacement

Roof Replacement

Tile-to-metal switch or full Colorbond replace. Done in 3-5 days for most Ballarat homes, fully removed and recycled - 10-year written warranty on workmanship.

02 / Restoration

Roof Restoration

Pressure clean, re-bed, re-point, and recoat. Adds 10-15 years to an existing terracotta or cement-tile roof at a fraction of replacement cost.

03 / Gutters

Gutters & Downpipes

Quad, half-round, or box. Seamless aluminium or Colorbond - fitted with stainless brackets and leaf guard, paired with new PVC or metal downpipes.

04 / Emergency

Storm & Emergency Repairs

Hail, wind, or fallen-branch damage - we tarp same-day, photograph for your insurer, and replace tiles or sheeting once the claim's lodged.

05 / Pointing

Re-pointing & Re-bedding

Flexible pointing in 6 colours - replaces failed mortar bedding without disturbing tiles below. Stops leaks at the ridge, valleys, and hips.

06 / Colorbond

Metal Roofing & Colorbond

Surfmist, Monument, Basalt - full Colorbond install over timber battens. Includes anti-condensation blanket, insulation upgrade, and standing-seam detail at all junctions.

How we work

A calmer way to commission a new roof.

From the first phone call to the warranty paperwork: four predictable steps, fixed pricing in writing, and the same foreman from quote to handover.

  1. 01

    On-site quote

    We inspect the roof in person, photograph problem areas, and email a fixed, line-item quote within 48 hours - no high-pressure follow-ups.

  2. 02

    Scheduling & materials

    A confirmed start date in writing. Colorbond profiles, gutter colours, and pointing shades chosen with a sample board at your kitchen table.

  3. 03

    On the tools

    Most Ballarat homes are done in 3-5 days. You get a daily update from the foreman and a tidy site every evening before the crew packs down.

  4. 04

    Sign-off & warranty

    Final walk-through with photos, manufacturer paperwork, and the 10-year workmanship warranty handed over the day we leave.
