02About & Method

Same crew on your roof from quote to clean-up.

EST. 2003 · VBA

Vicwest started in 2003 with a single ute and one rule: the crew that quotes the job is the crew that finishes it. Twenty-two years later, the rule still holds.

We are Victorian Building Authority licensed, fully insured, and family-run. We do not subcontract framing crews, gutter crews, or clean-up. The same faces you meet at the quote turn up on the roof and stand on the lawn the day they leave.

If you are between repairs and replacement, we will tell you which makes more sense for your roof — even when that means a smaller invoice for us.

The job, step by step.

06 STAGES
01

Free site inspection

We climb up, photograph every plane, and check battens, flashings, valleys, and gutters. You get the photos either way.

02

Written, itemised quote

Materials, hours, and warranty terms on one page. No verbal estimates, no “trust us on the day” pricing.

03

Scheduling around the weather

We watch the BoM forecast and book around it. If a job needs to be paused for rain, you hear from us, not silence.

04

On-site work

Drop sheets on the lawn, magnetic sweep at the end of every day, no offcuts in the garden bed. Daily progress update.

05

Final inspection together

We walk the boundary with you, point a hose at the trouble spots, and don’t pack the trailer until you sign off.

06

Warranty paperwork on the day

10-year written workmanship warranty in your hands before we leave. We are still the people who answer the phone in year nine.
