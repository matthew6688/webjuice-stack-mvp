Workflow

No surprises,
no hidden trades.

From the first phone call to the warranty paperwork in your hands, every job moves through four documented stages — so you know what's happening on your roof, on what day.

i.

Site inspection & written quote

We climb, photograph and measure — usually same week. You receive a written, itemised quote with the exact materials and warranty terms — no verbals.

ii.

Scheduling & material order

Once the quote's accepted, we lock a start date and order Colorbond, tiles, or hardware to your suburb directly. We confirm the day before we arrive.

iii.

On-site work & daily updates

Same crew on your roof, every day. Photo updates each evening, tidy site each morning. Any change order is documented and signed off before extra cost.

iv.

Sign-off & warranty

Final walk-around, before/after photographs, manufacturer paperwork and the 10-year written workmanship warranty handed to you the day we leave.
