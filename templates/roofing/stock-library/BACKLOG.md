## Quality bar (apply to every image you generate)

Reject + regenerate if:

- ❌ Visible face (unless prompt explicitly allowed back-only)
- ❌ Readable brand name / logo on truck / clothing / tools (Makita/DeWalt teal/yellow OK · readable text NO)
- ❌ Weird hands · 6 fingers · misshapen
- ❌ Text artifacts in image
- ❌ Wrong aspect ratio
- ❌ US suburban context (mailbox by curb · vinyl siding) · must be Australian
- ❌ Tropical / palm-tree context · must be temperate Aus (eucalyptus · brick veneer · Colorbond)
- ❌ AI-slop tells (plastic skin · floating objects · impossible angles · over-smooth surfaces)
