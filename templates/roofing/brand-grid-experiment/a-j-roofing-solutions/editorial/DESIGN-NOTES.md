# A & J Roofing Solutions — Editorial brand render

## What changed from the base V5 editorial template

The OD `editorial` magazine pattern is preserved end-to-end — sticky masthead with brand + folio, "Vol · Issue" eyebrow, serif H1/H2 with display ligatures, six numbered sections, plate captions, drop-cap About, letters page, suburb pill list, dark footer, mobile sticky CTA. What changed is the brand surface around it.

The palette swung from V5's Ballarat heritage cream (#fbf7f0 paper, #9a5a2f rust accent, slab Source Serif Pro for everything) to A & J's tropical Cairns kit: sand-cream surface (#F6F0E4) on a white card stack, tropical canopy green (#1B5E3F) as the eyebrow + folio + phone CTA, Coral Sea teal (#2A8C8C) carrying links and the secondary outline button, sun-bleached terracotta (#D9744A) used sparingly — one hero H1 word ("wet season"), proof-chip hot variant, and the mobile sticky "Call now" affordance. The footer flips to deep canopy shade (#0F3D2A).

Typography intentionally splits — Source Serif Pro stays on display H1/H2 to keep the magazine register, but body, masthead nav, chips, captions and form move to DM Sans 500/700/800. That tension (editorial serif headlines over a sans-serif tropical-trade body) is the "interesting friction" the brief asked for — a long-serving Cairns roofer with a magazine-quality table of contents.

Content is fully a-j: Cairns + Portsmith location, 07 4035 6187 phone, metal roofing + ceiling/wall cladding + residential/commercial + architectural detailing services, 15 FNQ suburbs in coverage. No fabricated testimonials — the "letters page" section honestly carries the verbatim 18+ year positioning quote with a "testimonials forthcoming" aside. No fake licence number — chips read "Licensed & Fully Insured · ABN 34 134 811 831". Hero and gallery images use brand-coloured gradient placeholders labelled "photography pending" so the YELLOW data-checkpoint provenance is visible, not hidden.
