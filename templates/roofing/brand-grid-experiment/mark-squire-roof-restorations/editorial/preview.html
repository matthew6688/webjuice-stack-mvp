Section I

The Catalogue

Five trades. One ledger.

Every job is inspected on site, costed in writing, and finished by the same crew that started it. No subcontractors, no surprises on the invoice.

01 / Restoration

Roof Restoration

High-pressure clean, re-bed and re-point ridge caps with flexible mortar, then a two-coat acrylic membrane. Adds years to tile and metal roofs across Ballarat at a fraction of replacement cost.

02 / Repairs

Roof Repair & Leak Detection

Active leaks, broken tiles, valley irons, ridge caps and sarking patches. Tarped same-day where it's safe, photographed for your records, then back to fix it once the weather lets us.

03 / Gutters

Gutter Replacement

BlueScope .48mm Colorbond with concealed brackets and 90mm downpipes. Flashings folded on site to match your pitch, valley angles and the existing roof line.

04 / Guard

Gutter Guard Systems

Aluminium mesh or steel ember guard, clipped every 300mm to handle Ballarat wind loads. Reduces fire risk in bushfire-prone zones and extends gutter life by stopping standing-water rust.

05 / Metal

Metal Roofing & Colorbond

Colorbond or marine-grade Zincalume — standing-seam and corrugated profiles for new builds, re-roofs and extensions. Anti-condensation blanket and insulation upgrade where the spec asks for it.

06 / Inspection

Free On-Site Inspection

Free roof walk and written quote within 48 hours. Itemised scope, materials by brand, dates and a fixed price valid for 30 days. No call centre — Mark turns up himself.

Section II

The Workshop

A Ballarat trade, owner-on-site.

Mark Squire Roof Restorations is a family-operated roofing outfit based at 4 Eureka Terrace, Ballarat East. We specialise in tile and metal restoration across the Central Highlands — and Mark himself is on every job, every day.

Most of the work is restoration: high-pressure clean, repair damaged tiles, re-bed ridge caps with flexible mortar, then a two-coat acrylic membrane that's rated for Victorian weather. Where a roof's past restoration we'll quote a full Colorbond or Zincalume re-roof — BlueScope materials, flashings folded on site, all the metalwork honest and visible.

No subcontractors. No call centre. You text Mark a photo, he comes and looks, you get a written quote inside two working days. Same crew start to finish — and the people who quoted the job are the ones doing it.
