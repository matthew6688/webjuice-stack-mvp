Section I

The Catalogue

Six trades, one ledger.

Every job is quoted in writing, scoped on site, and signed off when it is done. No surprises on the invoice — and the warranty is on the paperwork, not the handshake.

01 / Replacement

Roof Replacement

Tile-to-metal switch or full Colorbond replace. Done in 3-5 days for most Ballarat homes, fully removed and recycled — 10-year written warranty on workmanship.

02 / Restoration

Roof Restoration

Pressure clean, re-bed, re-point, and recoat. Adds 10-15 years to an existing terracotta or cement-tile roof at a fraction of replacement cost.

03 / Gutters

Gutters & Downpipes

Quad, half-round, or box. Seamless aluminium or Colorbond — fitted with stainless brackets and leaf guard, paired with new PVC or metal downpipes.

04 / Emergency

Storm & Emergency Repairs

Hail, wind, or fallen-branch damage — we tarp same-day, photograph for your insurer, and replace tiles or sheeting once the claim's lodged.

05 / Pointing

Re-pointing & Re-bedding

Flexible pointing in 6 colours — replaces failed mortar bedding without disturbing tiles below. Stops leaks at the ridge, valleys, and hips.

06 / Metal

Metal Roofing & Colorbond

Surfmist, Monument, Basalt — full Colorbond install over timber battens. Includes anti-condensation blanket, insulation upgrade, and standing-seam detail at all junctions.

Section II

The Workshop

Twenty-two years on Ballarat roofs.

Vicwest Roofing has worked the Ballarat region since 2003 — Colorbond replacements through the central suburbs, terracotta restorations on the older Soldiers Hill and Buninyong stock, and the steady stream of storm work that comes with a Central Highlands winter.

The shed sits on Icon Drive in Delacombe. We are VBA-licensed in Victoria under CDB-U 65938, run our own crew rather than subcontracting the trade out, and we put a written ten-year workmanship warranty in your hand the day the job finishes. Quotes are scoped on site, costed in writing, and held to.

We take after-hours emergency calls for hail, wind and branch damage — tarped same-day where it is safe to access, photographed for the insurer, and back on the program once the claim's lodged.
