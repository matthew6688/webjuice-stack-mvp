What we do

6 things we do well across Ballarat roofs.

From repairs through to full replacements — every job comes with a written quote, an ABN-backed invoice, and the warranty in writing. Fully insured.

Roof Replacements

Remove ageing, damaged, tiled, corrugated, asbestos cement, or metal roofs and install new Colorbond or metal roofing with sarking, anticon, battens, flashings, clean handover, and a 10-year workmanship warranty.

→

New Roofs

Design, estimate, schedule, and install Colorbond and metal roofs for new residential builds, extensions, unit developments, display homes, and builder-led projects.

→

Gutter & Pipe Replacement

Supply and fit Colorbond gutters, fascia, downpipes, and leaf-guard options to improve drainage and reduce water damage risk.

→

Metal Roofing Replacement/Re-roofing

Replace older roofing systems with modern metal roofing for homes and commercial sites, including tile-to-tin and tile-to-Colorbond conversions.

→

Architectural Claddings

Provide associated metal cladding and architectural cladding services for residential and commercial projects.

→

Colorbond Claddings

Install Colorbond cladding systems as part of metal roofing and external envelope projects.
