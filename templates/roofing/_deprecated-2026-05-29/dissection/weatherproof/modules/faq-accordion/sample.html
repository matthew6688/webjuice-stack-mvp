The cost of tile roof repairs can vary significantly depending on the scope of work required. Factors such as the extent of damage, the number of broken or slipped tiles, roof pitch and access, and whether additional work like valley replacements, ridge repointing, or waterproofing is needed will all influence the overall cost. Every roof is different, which is why we offer tailored quotes based on a thorough inspection. If you'd like us to take a look at your roof and provide a no-obligation quote, get in touch with our team today.
The cost to restore a roof depends on a range of factors, including the size and pitch of the roof, its current condition, the type of roofing material, and the specific work required. A full roof restoration typically involves pressure cleaning, replacing broken tiles, re-bedding and repointing ridge capping, applying protective sealers, and repainting — but every roof is different. Because no two jobs are the same, we provide tailored quotes based on a detailed roof inspection. Ready to find out what your roof needs? Contact us for a free quote.
