Pressure Washing

Deep cleaning that restores the roof surface before coating

Roof cleaning starts with a clear look at the surface: staining, lichen, faded tile colour, and gutter edges all show where the roof needs careful treatment. A detailed clean removes organic build-up and gives the roof a stronger base for any repair or restoration work that follows.

  • Removes mould, lichen, dirt, and streaking
  • Prepares the roof surface for repairs or coating
  • Improves street appeal before larger restoration work
