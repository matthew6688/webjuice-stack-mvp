Our Process

How We Paint Your Roof

Roof Inspection

We begin with a thorough inspection to assess the condition of your roof, identifying any damage, wear, or areas that need attention before work begins.

High-Pressure Clean

Using commercial-grade pressure washing equipment, we remove all dirt, moss, lichen, and built-up grime to prepare a clean surface for repairs and painting.

Repairs & Repointing

We replace any broken or cracked tiles, re-bed and repoint ridge caps, and seal all valleys and flashings to ensure your roof is watertight.

Prime & Seal

A professional-grade primer and sealer is applied to the entire roof surface, ensuring maximum adhesion and long-lasting protection for the topcoat.

Roof Membrane Application

Finally, we apply a durable, UV-resistant roof membrane in your chosen colour. This not only boosts street appeal but also adds a protective barrier against harsh weather.
