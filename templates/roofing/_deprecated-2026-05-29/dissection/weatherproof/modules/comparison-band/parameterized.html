Roof Restoration vs Roof Repairs

A roof restoration is a remodel of your existing roof including cleaning, painting, and fixing any minor cracked tiles. While a roof restoration involves your entire roof, a roof repair is more targeted to problems areas such as a roof leak. The best way to determine whether you need a roof repair, or a roof restoration is usually by the size of the damage or affected area. If you are unsure about what your roof needs, we can come out for a no obligation quote to best determine your needs.

How Much Do Tile Roof Repairs Cost? In the coating and roofing industry, when it comes to tile roofs the most frequent repair services involve replacing cracked tiles, fixing leaks and filling gaps. The cost of your tile roof repairs will be varied and can be affected by several factors:

  • Age of your roof
  • Size of the damaged area
  • Necessary repair supplies
  • Concrete cleaning
  • Accessibility of the area
