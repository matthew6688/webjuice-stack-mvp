About Roftrix Roofing

A roofing company that respects the home it works on.

We are a residential roofing team built around one idea: a roof is the most exposed, most expensive layer of a home, and homeowners deserve to understand what is happening up there before any work begins.

Our origins

Started by tradespeople who were tired of vague roof quotes.

Roftrix began on a simple complaint heard from homeowner after homeowner: the quote arrived, the work was done, and nobody could explain why it cost what it cost or what had actually been replaced. We set out to do the opposite. Every visit produces notes the homeowner can keep. Every scope item is named in plain language. Every recommendation is tied to a visible part of the roof.

That principle has not changed. The company has grown, the projects have grown larger, but the discipline of showing the work has stayed exactly where it started.

Founding philosophy

Three commitments we keep on every roof.

These are not slogans. They are the things we measure ourselves against at the end of each project.

Heritage over hurry

A home built in 1962 should not be repaired as if it were built last year. Material, profile, and detailing decisions begin with the architecture, not the catalogue.

Craft is visible

Ridge lines, flashing transitions, valley work, and trim cleanup are the parts that age slowest when done correctly. We treat them as the project, not the afterthought.

Clarity, not pressure

If a roof can wait two years, we will say so. If it cannot, we will explain exactly why. Homeowners make the call from real information, not a sales script.

Inside the work

Roofing is a craft, not a commodity.

Most of what makes a roof last is invisible from the street. Underlayment laps, fastener pattern, ridge ventilation, the way flashing wraps a chimney, the small choice of where a sheet ends and the next one begins. These are the decisions our crew is trained to make, and the ones we expect to be asked about.

We use the same crew on every project. There is no subcontracting of the actual roof work, which means the same eyes that scoped the job are the eyes finishing it. When something on site needs a small judgment call, the person making it knows the rest of the roof.

The team

The people who walk your roof.

Roftrix is small on purpose. Every project has a named lead, a named estimator, and a crew that has worked together long enough to anticipate each other.

The crew

Trained on residential profiles, ventilation, and the small detail work that separates a roof you notice from a roof that simply lasts.

The estimator

Walks the roof, then walks the homeowner through the scope. Quote language is written to be readable, not to hide options.

The workshop

Where material decisions are made. We keep samples on hand so homeowners can see, touch, and compare before any order goes in.

What we will not do

The work we politely decline.

We do not do high-volume insurance chasing, we do not pressure homeowners into replacement when a careful repair is what the roof needs, and we do not bid on jobs where the scope cannot be explained truthfully. There are roofing companies that work that way. We are not one of them.

The result is a slower calendar and a smaller business. We are comfortable with that trade.

Begin the conversation

The first step is a walk of the roof, not a sales call.

If something is changing on your roof — a stain on a ceiling, a missing piece of ridge cap, an age question before a sale — we will come look. The visit notes are yours to keep, whether or not we end up doing the work.
