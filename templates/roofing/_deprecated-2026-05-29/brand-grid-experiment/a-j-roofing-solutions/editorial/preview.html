Section II

The Workshop

Eighteen years on Far North Queensland roofs.

A & J Roofing Solutions has worked Cairns and Far North Queensland for over eighteen years — specialists in metal roofing, ceiling and wall cladding for the homes, builders and commercial sites that make up the region.

The workshop sits at 98 Buchan St, Portsmith. The team handles both residential and commercial work, with separate streams for each — from a single Queenslander re-roof through to commercial cladding briefs that involve architects, builders and project coordination.

The trade language matters: metal roofing systems, cladding details, and the kind of practical roofing knowledge that comes from eighteen years of FNQ climate. Licensed and fully insured.
