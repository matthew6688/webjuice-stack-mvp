# Mark Squire · Editorial brand-injection · DESIGN-NOTES

**Base**: `templates/roofing/single-page-library/_compare/v5-od-editorial/preview.html` (OD editorial design-system rendered with vicwest content, generic editorial palette).

**This render**: same magazine register — `Vol · Issue` eyebrow, Section folio numerals, plate captions, drop-cap opener, blockquote pull-quote — but every visual surface re-pointed onto Mark Squire's canonical brand kit.

## What changed

- **Surface palette** — vicwest's golden cream `#fbf7f0` swapped for Mark Squire's warm cream `#FAF6EE` (surface-muted). Hero/strap/cards now sit on white `#FFFFFF` (surface), pages wash to cream. The cream is brand-spec critical: it signals regional / honest / non-corporate.
- **Display type** — editorial Georgia/Source-Serif-Pro swapped for **Roboto Slab 800/600**, the slab-serif heritage font that's drawn into the MS monogram itself. Body type switched to **Inter** (sans). The editorial register survives the swap — slab serif actually carries the magazine voice better than the generic Georgia stack.
- **Accent / meta** — vicwest's terracotta `#9a5a2f` replaced with Mark Squire's **warm ochre `#C68C3F`** on H2 underlines, story-number folio, blockquote drop-caps, ★ rating glyphs, primary CTA, mobile-bar CTA, focus rings.
- **Secondary** — added Mark Squire's **slate-blue `#3A5A6B`** (the ridge-cap brand DNA) on links, masthead phone, secondary-button border. Header phone reads in slate-blue per brand-spec rule.
- **Logo** — header swaps the text wordmark for `logo-horizontal.svg`. Footer (charcoal `#1F232B`) carries `logo-mono-light.svg`. Favicon points at `favicon.svg`. Tabs and Google preview pick up the MS monogram.
- **Footer charcoal** changed from `#1f1a16` (warm vicwest dark) to brand-primary `#1F232B` (cool charcoal). Secondary text shifts to brand-spec `#B5B5B7`.
- **Radii** softened to 4px per brand-spec ("owner-friendly feel, slightly softer than VIP's sharp corners").

## What didn't change

The editorial scaffold: eight-section flow (hero → strap → catalogue → workshop → letters → plates → beat → correspondence → colophon), Vol/Issue eyebrow, Section I-VI folio numerals with hairline, drop-cap on the workshop opener, plate captions on hero + gallery, blockquote pull-quotes for reviews. That register is what the design-system contributes; the brand provides the surfaces and the type voice.
