Pick the steel profile that suits your roof pitch

Three Australian-standard Colorbond® profiles · each with a specific structural and aesthetic fit. We'll recommend on-site.

Trimdek®
Most common · residential
  • Pitch 2°+ minimum (low-pitch friendly)
  • Cover width 762mm per sheet
  • Rib depth 21mm trapezoidal
  • Gauge 0.42mm BMT residential
  • Best for Re-roofs · contemporary homes · garages
Klip-Lok®
Concealed-fix · architectural
  • Pitch 1°+ minimum (very low-pitch)
  • Cover width 700mm per sheet
  • Rib depth 41mm standing seam
  • Gauge 0.42mm or 0.48mm
  • Best for Modern flat-line roofs · no visible fasteners · long runs
Custom Orb®
Classic corrugated · heritage
  • Pitch 5°+ minimum
  • Cover width 762mm per sheet
  • Rib depth 16mm sinusoidal
  • Gauge 0.42mm BMT residential
  • Best for Federation · weatherboard cottages · sheds · period restorations
